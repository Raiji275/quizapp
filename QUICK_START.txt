🎯 HOW TO RUN:

1. Double-click "index.html" 
   OR
   Right-click "index.html" → "Open with" → Your web browser

2. The quiz will open in your default browser
3. Click "Start Quiz" to begin!

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

📝 QUIZ STRUCTURE:

• 10 Total Questions
• 4 Reading Comprehension (with passages)
• 3 Listening Questions (text-to-speech)
• 3 Writing Tasks (with word count)
• 5-minute timer
• Instant results & detailed review
